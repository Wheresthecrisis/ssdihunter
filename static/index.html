Rank (#1–#10)
Position in autocomplete dropdown. Google orders these by predicted CTR from its internal volume data — #1–3 = high demand, #4–6 = moderate, #7–10 = lower.
Frequency
How many of our ~53 query variations returned this keyword. Higher = Google keeps volunteering it across many searches = stronger underlying demand signal.
Signal Tier
Strong — Both sources confirmed + high freq or top rank
Moderate — Both sources, or solid single-source signal
Weak — Low confidence, single source, low freq
Match Type
Exact — ≤2 words, attorney/lawyer terms, Rep/Attorney intent, weak signal
Phrase — 3–4 words with commercial intent, moderate specificity
Broad — 5+ words, long-tail, self-filtering by specificity
Raw Score (f · r · s)
f = frequency  r = best rank  s = source count. Relative signal strength — not absolute search volume.
